Sum up 21 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $100: $20 for 1st place, $12 for 2nd, $6 for 3rd, $2 each for 4th to 10th and $1.20 each for 11th to 50th.

The entry is one editorial poster (1024×1024): the day's biggest story — Houthi missiles fired at Riyadh early on Saturday, three ballistic missiles intercepted — above three more: NATO chief Rutte's visit to Warsaw, the COP31 climate talks spilling into Saturday with no deal, and two oil tankers struck by unknown projectiles off Oman, with the Mocha port strike, seven hurt in Gaza strikes, Pakistan's condemnation of the KAFD strike and eight emergency alerts in the footer. The date is named in the masthead; every headline is drawn as crisp text so the image reads at any size.
