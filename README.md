Sum up 21 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $100: $20 for 1st place, $12 for 2nd, $6 for 3rd, $2 each for 4th to 10th and $1.20 each for 11th to 50th.

The entry is one editorial poster (1024×1024), rebuilt with the full Saturday's news: the day's biggest story — Iran's Revolutionary Guard claims it hit US Patriot radars in Bahrain (Bahrain says the site hit was civilian, a fire, no injuries) — above three more: the EU unveils a 3% levy on Big Tech, Wolbachia mosquitoes cut dengue in Brazilian children by 69%, and COP31 risks failure with no new text by Saturday evening, with the Riyadh missiles, two tankers off Oman, the Russian ship Sparta II ablaze, the Guard's claim of downing a US drone, seven hurt in Gaza strikes and Pakistan's condemnation of the KAFD strike in the footer. The date is named in the masthead; every headline is drawn as crisp text so the image reads at any size.
